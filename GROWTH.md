# Growth playbook — @modern.storiez

Instagram ranks Reels mainly on **watch %** (rewatch >100% is the strongest signal), then shares, saves, comments, likes.
Check `npm run dashboard` → "What's working" and the **Watched** column after every few posts.

## Rules every post follows
1. **Hook in 0–2 s.** Stories open with a question only the ending answers (`hook` in stories.json).
2. **Short.** Quotes ~7.5 s, health tips ~11 s, stories ~30 s (1.12× narration, 2.8 s end card).
3. **Loop.** Cards have no end screen and 0.25 s audio fades, so the replay is seamless.
4. **Delayed punchline.** Quote line 2 appears at ~42% of the video in gold → viewers replay to absorb it.
5. **CTA in the caption, not the video** (end screens break loops) — except stories, which keep a short end card.
6. **Ask a question in every caption** → comments. Reply to all comments within the first hour.
7. **Tags match the content** (category tag sets), ~8–10 tags, never unrelated trending tags.
8. **3–4 posts/day, ≥2 h apart.** Posting back-to-back splits the audience.

## Data so far (day 1, 0 followers — treat as early signals)
- Quote (9 s): ~2× rewatch, most views. Story (41 s, no hook): 30% watched → led to the changes above.

## Updated after researching Instagram's 2026 ranking (Oct 2026)
- **Max 5 hashtags** — Instagram demotes posts with more from Explore/Reels. All captions now use exactly 5.
- **Sends per reach** (DM shares) is the #1 signal for reaching non-followers → every caption asks to *send* it to someone.
- **Search keywords** in plain words in every caption (Instagram search reads captions).
- **Comment replies** every 30 min (`src/engage.js`), varied wording + a follow-up question.
- **Originality**: Instagram favours real human content in 2026. Biggest future upgrade: your own voice or face.
- **Trial Reels** (test on non-followers first) unlock around 1,000 followers — add then.
