# Privacy Policy — Modern Stories Autopost

_Last updated: 7 October 2026_

Modern Stories Autopost is a personal automation tool used only by the owner of the
"Modern Stories" Instagram account (@modern.storiez) and YouTube channel to publish their own videos.

- **What it accesses:** only the owner's own YouTube channel (to upload videos and read basic channel/video statistics) and the owner's own Instagram account (to publish posts and reply to comments).
- **What it stores:** sign-in tokens are kept as encrypted secrets in the owner's GitHub repository and local machine. No data about other people is collected, sold or shared.
- **Comments:** public comments on the owner's posts are read only to post replies; they are not stored beyond a list of comment IDs already answered.
- **Contact:** ad.stop084@gmail.com

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
